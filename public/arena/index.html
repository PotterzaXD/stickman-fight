How to play

Keyboard

A D or arrows: move

W / Space: jump (press again in the air to double jump)

S: drop through a plank

J or click: attack (chain 3 for a heavy finisher)

K: weapon skill

L: grab / swap weapon · Esc: pause

Touch & gamepad

Drag anywhere on the left half to spawn a joystick. Push it up to jump.

Buttons on the right: attack, skill, jump, grab.

Gamepad: stick to move, A jump, X attack, Y skill, B grab.

Rules of the page

You and bots have 100 HP. The boss has 1,000.

Fall off the page and you are out. Spikes hurt. Springs fling you.

Weapons appear on glowing spots. Touch one to take it.
